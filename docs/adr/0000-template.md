# NNNN. Short title naming the decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-other.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the failure, or the fork in the road — written so someone
who was not there understands why doing nothing was not an option. Name the alternatives that were
genuinely considered, not a strawman.

## Decision

What was chosen, in one or two sentences, in the active voice: "We store refresh tokens as SHA-256
hashes in `refresh_tokens`."

## Consequences

The section that matters most. What this buys, and — more importantly — **what it costs**, because
that is what tells a future reader what they are giving up if they change it.

- What becomes easy.
- What becomes hard or impossible.
- What now has to be remembered (an operational step, a rule a reviewer must check).
- What would have to be true for this decision to be worth revisiting.
